About

A UX/UI designer who also builds what she designs.

For over three years I've been designing and developing digital products end to end — from understanding a business and its customers, through wireframes and high-fidelity UI in Figma, to the live, responsive site. Design and code are one job to me: I don't hand over a pretty screen and hope for the best, I build it and make sure it behaves.

I work in HTML and CSS, WordPress and Elementor, and I treat AI tools — Claude among them — as a real part of the process: faster research and content structure, quicker prototypes, cleaner code, more time left for the actual design decisions.

My work spans e-commerce, personal brands, and local businesses, where clear structure and a confident visual voice make the difference between a website that looks nice and one that converts.
